If you're unsure whether your service should use a GOV.UK domain and header or a Planning Inspectorate domain and header, this guidance will help you make an informed decision.

Start by considering where your service will be hosted, how users will access it, and any agreed decisions from architecture, service governance or GDS. Once a domain has been agreed, the corresponding header pattern should be used consistently throughout the service.

When to consider a Planning Inspectorate domain

Questions to consider:

  • Is the service operated independently by the Planning Inspectorate?
  • Is the service acting as a specialist operational portal?
  • Has a decision already been made not to use a service.gov.uk domain?

Record the decision

Where a service does not use a GOV.UK domain, record:

  • The agreed domain
  • The rationale of the decision
  • Who made the decision
  • any advice received from GDS, architecture or design governance

What not to use as the sole decision criteria

The GOV.UK domain guidance I reviewed does not identify the following as deciding factors for whether a service should use a service.gov.uk domain:

  • Traffic volume
  • Number of users
  • Whether users are members of the public
  • Whether users are professionals

The guidance focuses on domain governance and use of appropriate government domains rather than service volume.
