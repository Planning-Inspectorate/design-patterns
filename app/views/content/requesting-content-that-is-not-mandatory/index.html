Do not make users stop their journey just because they do not have a document immediately available.

Use a simple yes or no question

Sometimes a user may not have access to a document or attachment while they are using a service. We want to ask them for it without making them stop the journey because they do not have it.

We also do not want to make it seem like the document is optional or unimportant. Research shows that users are less likely to attach a document when it is marked as optional, even if they already have it.

Recommended approach

  • Ask the user whether they have the document.
  • Give them Yes or No options to continue.
  • If they select Yes, route them to a screen asking them to upload it.
  • If they select No, route them to the next question in the journey.

This keeps the document request clear while making it easier for users to continue when they do not have it to hand.
